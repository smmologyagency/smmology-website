What to Post for Your Local Business When You Don't Know What to Post

By Diana De La O · November 2026

Give every post a job. Each post should do one of four things for your business: attract new people, engage the people who already follow you, convert them into leads, or help them buy. That's The Martini Method, the same strategy behind every tier at Smmology, and once each post has a job, "what do I post today" turns into a much smaller question.

Your business already has plenty to show. What's missing is a plan for what each post is for, right? So here's how I'd start.

Why posting whatever you throw together doesn't add up

Posting alone isn't a strategy. Without a goal and a way to measure it, a post has no purpose, and it turns into throwing spaghetti to a wall. Some of it sticks, and you never find out why.

Some owners post in a smart way without realizing it, and it works for them. For most of us, posting whatever looks good that day won't get the business where it wants to go.

The four jobs a post can do

  • Attract. Posts that reach people who have never heard of you.
  • Engage. Posts that build trust, a relationship and a community with the people who already follow you.
  • Convert. Posts that turn followers into leads, the people who reach out, book or sign up.
  • Buy. Posts that make it easy for a warm follower to become a customer.

When you plan your month, give each post one of these jobs before you film anything. It shows you right away which stage you've been skipping.

Four stories your business already has

You don't have to invent ideas from scratch. Every business has four stories to pull from:

  • The Origin Story: why you started.
  • The Method Story: how you do what you do.
  • The Client Journey: what it's like to work with you, from the first visit to the result.
  • The Belief Story: what you believe about your work.

Tell each one with a simple shape: before, the turning point, and after.

You don't need to post every day

The right content beats more content. You don't need to post daily, jump on every trend or go viral. A trend only makes sense when your strategy is built around it, and going viral can work against a local business when it brings the wrong audience.

If you'd rather not be on camera

You can show who you are without sharing your whole personal life, and without being on camera all the time. A voiceover over B-roll you already have on your camera roll gets your message across in your own voice.
