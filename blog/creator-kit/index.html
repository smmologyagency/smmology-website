The $100 Creator Kit
That Actually Works

Your phone is already your camera. What you need is great light and clean sound — and you can get both for less than a night out.

By Diana De La O · November 2026 · 6 min read · Beginner Friendly

Your camera is already in your pocket. If you're starting out, your phone is your camera — and that's not a compromise, that's the reality for most working creators. What you don't already have is great light and clean sound. Get those two things right and even an older phone produces content worth watching. That's the whole philosophy behind this kit.

01 — Lighting

A bad camera with great light beats a great camera with bad light. Every time.

Before spending anything — try filming in front of a window during golden hour, or find the best-lit spot in your home. Natural light is free and often genuinely better than anything you can buy. The ring light is for when you need to create on your own schedule, regardless of time of day or weather.

02 — Audio

Nobody forgives bad audio.
Nobody forgives blurry video either.

This is where most beginners underinvest. A $20 wired lavalier mic sounds dramatically better than your phone's built-in microphone — especially for talking-head content in spaces with echo. When you're ready to invest more, the DJI Mic Mini is the standard. The full kit comes with two transmitters, so you can use one for yourself and one for an interview guest, a co-host, or as a backup. That versatility is what makes the price-per-use worth it.
