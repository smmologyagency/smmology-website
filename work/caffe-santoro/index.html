Where it started

Nobody was managing Caffè Santoro's social media. Ken posted pictures himself, and the account didn't have a plan behind it.

What we did

  • Weekly Reels and posts planned with The Martini Method
  • A fun, comedic brand voice with a narrator persona named "Roxie"
  • Event coverage, employee spotlights and seasonal promotions
  • Redesign of the in-store TV menu display into a single grid in brand colors
  • Small monthly Meta awareness ads
  • Monthly reports and strategy, community management, editing and scheduling

The bigger shift

Ken went from posting pictures himself to having a brand with its own voice that customers comment on.

These are Caffè Santoro's results, not a promise for every business. What I commit to is working toward your goals, on strategy, adjusting from your analytics.
